This website is just a result of me realizing that I can make my pointless tangents public. For the longest time, I used to awkwardly share images over Discord or WhatsApp to show my friends something cool/interesting. I ended up forgetting most of what I'd made, until I went back through my messages one day, and came to notice the younger me was exploring stuff in surprising detail; detail that was wasted in a dead chat.


I hope to have that be organized neatly, here.

## What do I do?

I've had my hands in various jars, from writing excel scripts for my dad, to running a [youtube channel](https://youtube.com/c/thatcontroluser) <sub>(~30k subs)</sub> that funded my first actual desktop PC, all the way to reverse engineering old games, writing simple gameboy emulators, automating roblox minigames using OpenCV, to writing services handling **_millions_** of requests for popular games like [Steal a Brainrot](https://www.roblox.com/games/105626692504093/Be-a-Brainrot) and [Nuke Your City](https://www.roblox.com/games/113918641206373/Nuke-Your-City).

> [!CAUTION]
> You'd be surprised by the amount of traffic these roblox games can generate. [Steal a Brainrot](https://www.roblox.com/games/105626692504093/Be-a-Brainrot) generated around 20-30 million requests in a week to a simple reward claim system. The kind of scale I never expected when I first took up the gig.
>
> <a href="google.com"><sub>(You can read about how I handled that nightmare by not handling it at all, here.)</sub></a>

I have always had a fascination with Game Engines and specifically various rendering tricks that they use to fool the average person into thinking a flat screen contains a 3D world within it. I always strive to learn a little bit more about these tricks, slowly building up a repertoire. 

I've got ~2 years under my belt, a fledgling by all means, but a very eager one. This time was split across freelance work and internships during my college days for [<i>Classify</i>](https://classify.org.uk), and [<i>Vector Consulting Group</i>](https://www.vectorconsulting.in/en/). Learned a significant bit from them, especially the illusion of seamless cross-platform nature of *Flutter* at Classify, or the perils of working with a **BaaS** like _Firebase_.
